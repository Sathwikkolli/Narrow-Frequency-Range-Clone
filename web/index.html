Here is the twist

Every single clip was fake. There was no real voice to find.

All three were the same sentence, cloned by three different AI systems, then level-matched and resampled so loudness and audio quality could not tip you off. One was built to be catchable. Two were not.

So why does our lab care?

If ears cannot do this reliably, detection has to be a measurement, not an opinion. We train models on the spectrogram instead of the sound, and we study what survives a phone call: squeeze a clip into the 300–3400 Hz telephone band at 8 kHz and most of the high-frequency evidence a detector relies on is simply gone. A detector that scores well on studio audio can fall apart on a voicemail, and voicemails are exactly where these scams land.
